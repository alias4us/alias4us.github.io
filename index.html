About

Alias4us is the story of FX and Fenni, whose musical journey began during their university years in Pisa around the turn of the millennium. Although life led them down different and demanding professional paths, songwriting remained a constant companion. For more than twenty-five years they kept writing, collecting melodies, lyrics and emotions inspired by love, loss, hope and the unexpected turns of life.

Today, those songs finally come to life through Alias4us, where human creativity meets modern technology. Blending acoustic rock, classic rock, progressive influences and cinematic atmospheres, their music is driven by authentic stories rather than trends.

Every song begins the same way: with a real melody, played on a guitar or a piano. Artificial intelligence didn't write their songs—it simply became the instrument that allowed decades of ideas to be heard through modern production, remixing and remastering.

Alias4us is proof that some dreams don't fade with time; they simply wait for the right moment to find their voice.
